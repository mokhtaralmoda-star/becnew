What is covered?

Comprehensive Insurance from BCare is your smart first choice if you’re looking for the cheapest comprehensive insurance in Saudi Arabia that combines competitive pricing with full protection. It provides complete coverage for your car, ensuring repair or replacement in case of an accident, even if you are 100% at fault. Additionally, it includes third-party liability coverage, allowing you to renew your vehicle registration instantly with automatic connection to the Najm and Absher systems. This policy also covers damages from floods, rain, and natural disasters, protects your car from fire and theft, and provides personal accident compensation for the driver and passengers. Optional features include agency repairs and a replacement car to ensure your complete convenience. Because we care about your savings, you can easily pay your insurance in installments through Tabby. You’ll also receive roadside assistance to secure your trip against any emergency. This type of coverage is the “recommended coverage” as it protects you from fines for lack of insurance and saves you from costly repairs.

Premium Insurance covers damages to others (both property and bodily) with a deductible, but it differs from regular “Third-Party” insurance with features that give you peace of mind—such as higher coverage limits, various additional benefits, broader case coverage, and a lower price than comprehensive insurance. The most important advantage is that the coverages for this product are diverse and customized based on the insurance company you choose, such as (Third-Party Plus), (Smart Insurance), or (Sanad Plus), providing the basic third-party insurance coverage with extra tailored benefits that enhance your protection and your car’s protection at minimal cost.

BCare’s Third-Party Insurance is the cheapest mandatory and approved car insurance in the Kingdom. It gives you peace of mind, keeps you free from insurance fines and automated monitoring tickets, and ensures full legal and financial protection in line with the regulations of the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) and traffic authorities so you can easily renew your vehicle registration. Under the unified motor insurance policy, the insurance company assumes full responsibility toward third parties, covering the repair costs of damaged vehicles, medical expenses, and legal claims that can reach up to 10 million SAR. It also guarantees instant automatic connection to the Najm system and the Absher platform in just one minute. Besides being the lowest-priced third-party insurance, you can add benefits such as roadside assistance to ensure your trips are always safe, compliant, and worry-free.
